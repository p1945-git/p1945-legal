P1945 Reporting — Data Deletion Instructions

Operator: PT Karya Indonesia Maju Oetama (Project 1945) · Contact: info@project1945.id

If you have interacted with our Instagram or Threads accounts and want your data deleted:

  1. You: email info@project1945.id with the subject "Data deletion request" and your username.
  2. We: delete all data about you held in our systems.
  3. Within 30 days: we reply to confirm the deletion.

Remove access at any time: an account owner can remove P1945 Reporting's access in Instagram settings (Apps and websites) or Threads settings (Website permissions).

Personal data is processed in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022). Full policy: legal.project1945.id/privacy

Instruksi Penghapusan Data

Pernah berinteraksi dengan akun Instagram atau Threads kami dan ingin data Anda dihapus:

  1. Anda: kirim email ke info@project1945.id dengan subjek "Data deletion request" dan username Anda.
  2. Kami: menghapus semua data tentang Anda di sistem kami.
  3. Maksimal 30 hari: kami membalas email Anda sebagai konfirmasi.

Pemilik akun dapat mencabut akses P1945 Reporting kapan saja di pengaturan Instagram (Apps and websites) atau Threads (Website permissions). Data pribadi diproses sesuai UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi.
